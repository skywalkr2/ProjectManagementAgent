Practice for bringing records in line after a date moves or a fact is replaced (core playbook):

- Follow the link first. An item that names the calendar entry (or its series) belongs to it, so what moved for the entry
  moved for the item. Words that match an old day are weaker: check that the record is about the same thing before
  changing it.
- Move a day by what it was for. Work due on the entry's day moves with it; work due well before it was due for its own
  reasons, and the new day may not change that.
- Keep to the smallest change. The new day or the corrected words, nothing else: owners, status and notes stay as the
  person left them.
- Whatever already reached someone is corrected by asking them, never edited behind their back. The correction says what
  changed, from what to what, and what you need from them.
- A move that leaves a record already right needs no change. Say nothing rather than rewrite it.
- When a fact was replaced, the newer fact is the latest word. A record that repeats the older one is brought in line;
  one that is only close in wording is left.
- An inferred change is proposed, never applied alone: if the move rests on something worked out and not said, leave
  the dates as they are and say so in a note.
