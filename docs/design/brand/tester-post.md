"I'll send that Friday."

Easy to say. Surprisingly easy to lose somewhere between three clients and a dentist appointment.

I'm building Thimbleby for independent professionals juggling work and personal life, and I'd love some help testing it.

Reginald, its helper, remembers what you promised and what others promised you. He drafts the follow-up and waits for
your yes before acting.

It's a Mac and Windows desktop app that runs on your own computer. You bring your own AI account, and your mail goes only
to that account.

A few heads-ups for this early test:
- the build is unsigned, so setup includes clicking through a security prompt once;
- if you connect Gmail, you'll reconnect it once a week during the test;
- please only connect work mail if your employer allows outside apps.

The ask: use it for two weeks and complete a short feedback form. Honest criticism very welcome. Places are limited.

To join, send me a LinkedIn message and I'll send the details.
