# Thimbleby: store and download page copy (draft, 2026-10-17)

Drafted with an outside assistant at the owner's request (three versions) and combined here. Kept honest against the
design: everything outward waits for the person's yes (6.32, phase S1b), and "on your own computer" never implies the
mail stays on it, because a run sends what it needs to the AI account the person chose (a local model keeps it local).
Features named here are phase S's; the copy ships with the installers (phase I), when they are built.

## Chosen draft

**Tagline:** You've got a lot going on. Reginald remembers.
**Subhead:** A little order. Nothing sent without you.

You promised a client the proposal on Friday. Someone promised you feedback by Tuesday. Somewhere in between is a dentist
appointment. Thimbleby helps independent professionals keep hold of it all. Its helper, Reginald, follows commitments
across your mail, calendar and notes, spots overdue replies and shifting plans, and drafts whatever needs to happen next.
He waits for your yes on the exact message or change before anything goes out or moves. Thimbleby runs on your own
computer, and your mail goes only to the AI account you choose. A helpful memory, with rather good manners.

- **Remember your word:** promises you made in sent mail and notes, found before they slip.
- **Remember theirs, too:** what others owe you, including the parts still missing.
- **Read past "sounds great":** a reply that leaves the real question unanswered is noticed.
- **Prepare, then ask:** replies, follow-ups and calendar changes ready for your approval.
- **Your computer, your AI account:** it runs on your machine, with the account you choose.
