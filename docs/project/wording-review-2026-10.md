## Notes

- The current `sources` / “source” wording appears in several related entries; the suggested “connection” wording is intended as one consistent replacement, not a new concept.
- “AI model” may be familiar to some testers but remains technical for others. “AI choice” is less precise; confirm that it still describes the setting before applying those suggestions.
- Some strings describe advanced add-on setup. If the intended audience is developers, keep technical setup terms there and move those screens out of the ordinary setup path.
