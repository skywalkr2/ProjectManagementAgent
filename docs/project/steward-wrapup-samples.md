# Evening wrap-up and Monday briefing samples

Samples for phase S2 (design 6.32), for an independent professional with fictional data. Drafted with an outside
assistant and rewritten here to the brief's rules (`docs/project/steward-brief-samples.md`): one fact per line, plain
words, an empty section left out, a quiet day as one line, and a line that needs the person's yes starting with what
they do and naming the thing. They become evaluation cases once the wrap-up and the briefing are built.

## The evening wrap-up

Three sections, in order: **Done today**, **Moved to tomorrow**, **Still waiting on others**. A done line credits the
work to whoever did it (the person, or someone who delivered). A waiting line says who owes what and when it's due. A
quiet day is the one line "Nothing carries over to tomorrow."

## The Monday briefing

The week ahead in five sections, in order: **Your promises this week**, **Promised to you**, **Deadlines**, **Clashes**,
**Needs your decision**. A promise or deadline line names what and by when; a clash line says what overlaps and by
how much; a decision line starts with what the person does. Empty sections are left out.

### Briefing 1

**Your promises this week**
- The Alder Circle proposal to Mira, by Wednesday.

**Promised to you**
- Oren's measurements, by Tuesday.

**Deadlines**
- The unused studio booking can be cancelled free until Thursday.

**Clashes**
- Wednesday's review overlaps your dentist appointment by 30 minutes.

**Needs your decision**
- Ask to move the review to 2:30 (draft ready).

### Briefing 2
