# O5: Second-opinion review of the daily steward's design

**Why:** a fresh reader finds the gaps the people who wrote a design no longer see.

**Read:** design section 6.32 in `docs/design/06-engine.md` (search "6.32"), `docs/project/steward-proposal.md`,
`docs/project/steward-scenarios.md`, and the roadmap's "Phase S" (`docs/project/roadmap-to-1.0.md`).

**Answer:**
1. Where would a non-technical person be confused or lose trust in their first week?
2. Which promise endings, reminders or brief lines could be wrong in a way the person wouldn't notice until too late?
   For each, how the design catches it, or that it doesn't.
3. What the design assumes about mail, calendars or the person's habits that may not hold for a freelancer, a small
   business owner or a parent.
4. Anything in the design that contradicts another part of it.
5. The three changes you would make first, and why.

**Produce:** `docs/project/reviews/steward-design-review-2026-10.md`. Cite the section or line for each point. Say
plainly where you are unsure.

**How it is checked:** the main session verifies each point against the design and the code, and puts the accepted ones
to the owner.
