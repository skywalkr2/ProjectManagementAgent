# Morning brief samples

Ten sample morning briefs for phase S2 (design 6.32), for an independent professional with fictional data. Drafted
with an outside assistant and rewritten here to the brief's rules. They show what the wording sheet and the brief's
prompt must produce, and they become evaluation cases once the brief is built.

## The rules these follow

- **Three sections, in this order:** what changed overnight, what is at risk today, what needs your yes. Headings come
  from the wording sheet: "Changed overnight", "At risk today", "Needs your yes".
- **A changed or at-risk line states the fact plainly**, one fact per line, naming the person and the thing. No filler
  verb ("Note that…", "Remember that…") in front of it.
- **An at-risk line says when it bites** (a time, a day, or "today"), and why it is at risk (not sent yet, late, a
  clash, more work than time).
- **A needs-your-yes line starts with what the person does** and names the thing itself ("Send Oren your estimate"),
  with what is ready beside it ("draft ready"). Its button opens the exact content; nothing goes out from the brief.
- **An empty section is left out.** A section never says it has nothing, and no line counts zero things.
- **A quiet day is one line:** "Nothing needs you this morning." No cheerful filler.
- **No status or progress lines:** "Three to-dos done yesterday" is not a brief line.
- **The capacity check** (S2) appears as an at-risk line when the day's to-dos need more time than the calendar has
  free, with the yes lines proposing what to move. Nothing is moved without the yes.

## Brief 1, Monday

**Changed overnight**
- Mira moved the draft deadline from Tuesday to Thursday.

**At risk today**
- The estimate you promised Oren is due at noon and not sent yet.

**Needs your yes**
- Send Oren your estimate (draft ready).

## Brief 2, Tuesday

**Changed overnight**
- Lena sent the signed agreement.

**At risk today**
- Dev's measurements are a day late.

**Needs your yes**
- Ask Dev when the measurements will arrive (draft ready).

## Brief 3, Wednesday

**Changed overnight**
- Tess asked to move your call to Friday at 10.

**At risk today**
- Friday at 10 clashes with your dentist appointment.

**Needs your yes**
- Offer Tess Friday at 11 instead (draft ready).

## Brief 4, Thursday

**Changed overnight**
- Mira sent two of the three drawings she promised.

**At risk today**
- The third drawing is still missing, and your review is at 2.

**Needs your yes**
- Ask Mira for the last drawing before 1 (draft ready).

## Brief 5, Friday

Nothing needs you this morning.

## Brief 6, Monday

**Changed overnight**
- Lena added a client review at 3.

**At risk today**
- Your to-dos need about three hours; your calendar has 30 free minutes.
- The proposal you promised to send today is one of them.

**Needs your yes**
- Move sorting the receipts to Wednesday.
- Ask Oren to move your call to tomorrow (draft ready).

## Brief 7, Tuesday

**Changed overnight**
- Your studio renewal letter says Friday is the last day to cancel.

**At risk today**
- You haven't decided whether to renew, and Friday is three days away.

**Needs your yes**
- Add a reminder for Thursday morning to decide on the renewal.

## Brief 8, Wednesday

**Changed overnight**
- Dev replied, but didn't say whether the quote includes travel.

**At risk today**
- Your budget review is at noon, and the quote may be incomplete.

**Needs your yes**
- Ask Dev whether the quoted price includes travel (draft ready).

## Brief 9, Thursday

**Changed overnight**
- Oren cancelled the workshop.

**At risk today**
- The room booking can be cancelled free only until noon.

**Needs your yes**
- Ask the venue to cancel the room (draft ready).

## Brief 10, Friday

Nothing needs you this morning.
